### 1) Summary
- **Family-Friendly Focus**: This itinerary is designed to cater to family fun and activities, ensuring kids have a memorable experience.
- **Legoland Johor Highlight**: The trip emphasizes a full day of excitement at Legoland Johor, along with other nearby attractions.
- **Budget-Conscious Plan**: The total trip budget of $2000 is carefully allocated to ensure comfortable stays, meals, and activities.

---

### 2) Day-by-Day Itinerary

**Day 1: Arrival in Iskandar Puteri**
- **Morning**: Arrive at Senai International Airport and transfer to hotel in Iskandar Puteri (approx. 30 minutes).
- **Accommodation**: Check into a family-friendly hotel (e.g., Legoland Hotel or nearby resorts).
- **Afternoon**: Explore the area around the hotel; visit Puteri Harbour for lunch at a kid-friendly restaurant (e.g., The Reading Room).
- **Evening**: Enjoy a stroll at Puteri Harbour, take photos with the marina view, and dinner at a local eatery. 
- **Overnight**: Rest at the hotel.

**Day 2: Legoland Johor**
- **Morning**: Breakfast at the hotel, then head to Legoland Johor (opening hours: 10 AM).
- **All Day**: Enjoy the various attractions, including rides, shows, and water park (bring swimsuits for the water park).
- **Lunch**: Eat at one of the park's family-friendly restaurants (budget for meals).
- **Evening**: Continue enjoying the park until closing (usually around 7 PM).
- **Dinner**: Return to the hotel area and have dinner at a family-friendly restaurant.
- **Overnight**: Rest at the hotel.

**Day 3: Visit Hello Kitty Town & Sanrio Hello Kitty Town**
- **Morning**: Breakfast at the hotel, then visit Hello Kitty Town (around 10 AM).
- **All Day**: Explore Hello Kitty Town, enjoy interactive and fun activities for children.
- **Lunch**: Lunch inside the park at a cafe; try themed food.
- **Afternoon**: Visit the adjacent Thomas Town for more fun.
- **Evening**: Return to the hotel to relax, followed by dinner at a local restaurant.
- **Overnight**: Rest at the hotel.

**Day 4: Shopping and Departure**
- **Morning**: Breakfast at the hotel, check out, and head to Johor Premium Outlets for shopping.
- **Lunch**: Lunch at the outlet mall (family-friendly food options available).
- **Afternoon**: Free time for shopping; enjoy some last-minute souvenir hunting.
- **Evening**: Depart for Senai International Airport for your flight home.

### 5) Safety Tips
- **Supervision**: Always keep an eye on children, especially in crowded areas like theme parks.
- **Hydration**: Ensure everyone stays hydrated, especially during outdoor activities.
- **First Aid**: Carry a small first aid kit with band-aids, antiseptic wipes, and any necessary medications.
- **Emergency Contacts**: Have a list of local emergency numbers and hotel contact information.
- **Sun Protection**: Apply sunscreen frequently, and wear hats and sunglasses to protect from sunburn.
- **Transportation Safety**: Use reputable transport services and always buckle up in vehicles.
